\paragraph{What it does to the comparison.} The axis is swept like the others. Both GRAIL arms
were rebuilt on the identical substrates, presented as the declared standardiser draws them, with
the same checkpoints, the same rule budgets, the same pool cap and the same ranking; the pools stay
keyed by the corpus string, so the annotation is one annotation and the two drawings are paired
substrate by substrate. Table~\ref{tab:si-dialect} gives the paired effect on each arm at each
budget. The largest anywhere is \numDsweepExhLargest{} for the exhaustive mode and
\numDsweepInterLargest{} for the interactive one, and \numDsweepExhSeparating{} of
\numDsweepExhBudgets{} and \numDsweepInterSeparating{} of \numDsweepInterBudgets{} cells separate
from zero.

Two properties of that measurement are worth stating before it is read. The stored column
reproduces the comparison table of the main text at every one of its cells, which is the check
that the sweep is measuring the same quantity and not a near neighbour of it; and pointing both
dialects at the same pools returns exactly zero everywhere with every interval collapsed, which is
the check that the pairing is right. A sweep that cannot show a non-difference as zero would hide
its own defect inside a plausible number.

Carried to the verdicts, the drawing moves \numDsweepMoved{} of \numDsweepCells{}
arm-against-comparator cells. The coverage ceiling on this population is \numDsweepCeilingstored{}
under the stored drawing and \numDsweepCeilingdrawn{} under the standardiser's, a difference of
$\numDsweepCeilingdiff$, $95\%$ CI $[\numDsweepCeilinglo, \numDsweepCeilinghi]$. It is measured
here rather than on the full evaluated split because the uncapped exhaustive pool exists in both
drawings for this population and not for that one.

Three comparators are held at the corpus drawing, because MetaTox is a web service and
MetaPredictor a frozen delivery and neither can be re-run here. A difference against them
therefore measures how much of our own margin was the drawing, which is the question that matters
for reading the comparison; SyGMa, which can be re-run, is swept above.
