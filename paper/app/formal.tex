The architecture, the coverage ceiling and the recall decomposition described in the main text
follow from one compact generative model, which also gives the three propositions of
Appendix~\ref{app:props} a common home. The model is a single likelihood together with an exact
decomposition of recall and a map from each diagnostic to the factor it constrains.

\paragraph{A latent-reaction mixture.}
For a substrate $s$, a metabolite $m$ arises by choosing a transformation rule, choosing a site at
which it fires, and applying it:
\[
P(m \mid s) \;=\; \sum_{r} \sum_{\text{site} \,\in\, \text{sites}(r,s)}
P(r \mid s) \cdot P(\text{site} \mid r, s) \cdot
\mathbb{1}\big[\text{apply}(r, s, \text{site}) = m\big].
\]
Application is deterministic, so the indicator collapses to the enumerable support of products.
The latent variables are which rule fired and where; the observation, an annotated metabolite set,
is positive-unlabelled, since a rule-applicable product without an annotation is unlabelled rather
than a certified negative.

The three deployed stages approximate the marginal likelihood of this model. The generator
approximates $P(r\mid s)$, and the frequency prior it stores is the marginal probability $\pi(r)$
that a rule fires. Rule application realises the deterministic support, enumerating the candidate
products. The filter approximates a discriminative correction $P(\text{true}\mid s,m)$ over those
candidates, and deployment ranks by the product of the filter and generator scores. Training under
a positive-unlabelled objective approximates expectation-maximisation over the unobserved
rule-firing indicator. This describes the model the pipeline approximates; it does not assert that
the trained weights are its maximum-likelihood solution.

\paragraph{An exact decomposition of recall.}
Fix a cut-off $k$. For substrate $s$ let $Y_s$ be its references, and let the candidate sets nest,
$R_s(k) \subseteq P_{\text{bud},s} \subseteq P_{\text{full},s}$, where $P_{\text{full},s}$ is the
pool reachable by applying the whole bank once, $P_{\text{bud},s}$ the pool the generator's budget
admits, and $R_s(k)$ the ranked output. Write $\text{hit}(A_s)$ for the number of references
matched by a set under the tautomer-aware quotient, which is monotone under inclusion, and pool
across substrates: $|U|=\sum_{s}|Y_s|$, $C_{\text{full}}=\sum_s\text{hit}(P_{\text{full},s})$,
$C_{\text{bud}}=\sum_s\text{hit}(P_{\text{bud},s})$ and $H=\sum_s\text{hit}(R_s(k))$. Then
\begin{align*}
\text{recall@}k &= \frac{H}{U}
= \left(\frac{C_{\text{full}}}{U}\right)
\cdot \left(\frac{C_{\text{bud}}}{C_{\text{full}}}\right)
\cdot \left(\frac{H}{C_{\text{bud}}}\right)
= \text{coverage} \cdot \text{selection} \cdot \text{ranking}.
\end{align*}
Because the sets nest, every factor lies in $[0,1]$ and the three multiply to the realised recall
on any numbers. This is an accounting identity rather than a theorem, and its use is to localise
where recall is lost. The coverage factor coincides with the rule-bank ceiling reported in the
main text.
