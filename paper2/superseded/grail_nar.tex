\title{\vspace{-2em}\Large\bfseries GRAIL: rule-grounded prediction of xenobiotic metabolite
structures with a fully declared evaluation}
\author{\normalsize AUTHORS\thanks{To whom correspondence should be addressed.}\\[-0.3em]
\small\itshape AFFILIATIONS}
\date{}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Predicting the metabolite structures a xenobiotic is converted into supports toxicology, drug
design and environmental risk assessment. Existing tools report a recall figure computed under
evaluation choices their papers do not state, so their numbers are not comparable to one another.
We present GRAIL, a rule-grounded predictor that names the transformation rule behind every
prediction, ranks its entire output, and reports its performance under five matching criteria and
eleven output budgets against every comparator with predictions on the same substrates.

On \numPopulationN{} test substrates the comparison divides by budget rather than resolving into
an ordering. At the tightest budgets SyGMa leads, at $k=1$ by \numSweepSygmaOne{} against
\numSweepTrainedOne{} with the paired interval excluding zero; in the middle MetaPredictor leads
without separating; from $k=15$ GRAIL leads, and at $k=30$ and $k=50$ its interval excludes zero
against every comparator. The advantage is at depth: the comparators saturate on lists of
\numOutputMetapredictor{} and \numOutputSygma{} candidates while GRAIL continues to have
candidates to rank. Against MetaTox alone, the incumbent web service, GRAIL leads at all nine
budgets. The server offers an interactive mode (median \numModeInteractiveMedian~s per substrate)
and an exhaustive mode (\numModeExhaustiveMedian~s). We additionally report the ceiling of the
approach: \numCeilingUncovered{} of \numPopulationTestrefs{} reference metabolites lie outside
what the rule bank reaches in one step, and we show this bound is invariant to how a
transformation type is defined, making it a property of the annotation corpus rather than of our
implementation.

\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent\textbf{Availability:} SERVICE\_URL; code, split, frozen predictions and evaluation
harness at \texttt{github.com/doctawho42/GRAIL}.
\end{abstract}
